Gene Petrella, Ph.D.
Discovery & Platform Biology Executive · Target-to-Candidate Drug Discovery · Therapeutic Strategy & Program Conception
Drug discovery leader with 25+ years advancing programs from target identification and
validation through hit discovery, lead optimization, IND-enabling pharmacology, and
candidate nomination. My foundation is protein structure/function and the biochemical and
biophysical methods used to characterize protein-ligand and RNA-ligand interactions, paired
with large-scale high-throughput screening and translational pharmacology.
Former Head of Discovery (Neutrolis), Sr. Director, Platform & Innovation
(Wave Life Sciences), and Investigator III (Novartis NIBR). Co-inventor of
ThermoFluor® (differential scanning fluorimetry). Founder of Alpine Start Bio,
a drug development advisory firm serving pre-seed through Series A biotechs.

How I work with computation
I conceive and direct ML-driven discovery workflows, including deep-learning protein and
sequence design, structure prediction, and in-silico screening, and I work hands-on with the
model outputs to make the scientific go/no-go decisions that move programs forward. My
contribution is scientific judgment and program strategy: choosing targets, building and
defending the rationale, weighing the failure modes of current therapeutics against real
areas of unmet need, and deciding what advances. I partner with computational scientists and
tooling to implement the pipelines.
